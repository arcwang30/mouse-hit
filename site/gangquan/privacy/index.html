隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

簡單來說：我們不收集你的個人資料。遊戲的設定與進度只存在你的裝置上，我們看不到。購買由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資訊。

1. 適用範圍

本政策適用於 Arc Concept Games（以下稱「我們」）開發的 iOS／iPadOS 遊戲 App《鋼拳風雲錄》（以下稱「本遊戲」）。

2. 我們不收集個人資料

  • 本遊戲不需要註冊帳號，也不會要求你提供姓名、電子郵件、電話等資料。
  • 本遊戲沒有廣告，也沒有使用分析、追蹤或廣告相關的第三方工具。
  • 本遊戲不會連線到我們的伺服器（我們沒有任何接收資料的伺服器），也不會追蹤你在其他 App 或網站上的活動。
  • 本遊戲所需的圖片、聲音與字型都內附在 App 裡，離線也能遊玩。

3. 存在你裝置上的資料

為了讓遊戲正常運作，以下資料只會存在你的裝置上：

  • 設定（音量、語言、震動、外觀等）
  • 遊玩進度（關卡紀錄、星數、成長點數、成就、每日登入與任務）
  • 金幣、道具（例如浴火重生），以及商店收藏（造型、桌布、圖鑑）

這些資料不會傳送給我們。存檔會另外備份在 App 自己的資料夾中，可能會隨你的 iCloud 或電腦裝置備份一起保存，這部分由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理。刪除 App 會刪除裝置上的存檔；如果沒有裝置備份，已存在存檔中的金幣與進度也無法找回。

4. App 內購買

金幣包的付款完全由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。我們不會收到你的信用卡、付款資訊或 Apple ID。本遊戲只會向 App Store 查詢商品價格並接收交易完成的通知，用來在你的裝置上增加金幣。Apple 如何處理你的資料，請參閱 Apple 隱私權政策。

金幣包屬於可重複購買的消耗型項目，購買後的金幣記錄在存檔中，無法透過 App Store 的「恢復購買」找回。

5. 外部連結

遊戲內有前往我們 Facebook 粉絲團的連結。只有在你點選時才會以瀏覽器開啟外部網頁，該網站適用其本身的隱私權政策。

6. 兒童

本遊戲不會收集任何人的個人資料，包括兒童。遊戲內有卡通風格的格鬥畫面，適合的年齡請參考 App Store 的年齡分級。

7. 政策變更

如果本政策有所變更，我們會更新本頁面與上方的生效日期。

8. 聯絡我們

對本政策有任何問題，請寄信至 arcconceptgames@gmail.com。

In short: We don't collect your personal data. Your settings and progress stay on your device, and we can't see them. Purchases are handled by Apple, and we never receive your payment information.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS/iPadOS game app Steel Fist: Wind & Cloud developed by Arc Concept Games ("we", "the Game").

2. We don't collect personal data

  • The Game doesn't require an account and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or similar information.
  • The Game has no ads and uses no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising tools.
  • The Game never connects to our servers (we don't run any server that receives data), and we don't track your activity in other apps or websites.
  • All images, sounds, and fonts the Game needs are included in the app, so it works offline.

3. Data stored on your device

To make the Game work, the following data is stored only on your device:

  • Settings (volume, language, vibration, appearance, and so on)
  • Play progress (stage records, stars, growth points, achievements, daily login and missions)
  • Coins, items (such as Phoenix), and shop collection (skins, wallpapers, codex)

This data is never sent to us. A backup copy of your save is kept in the app's own folder and may be included in your iCloud or computer device backups, which Apple handles under its privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes the save on your device; without a device backup, the coins and progress in that save can't be recovered.

4. In-app purchases

Payments for coin packs are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never receive your credit card, payment details, or Apple ID. The Game only asks the App Store for product prices and receives a notification when a purchase completes, so it can add coins on your device. For how Apple handles your data, see the Apple Privacy Policy.

Coin packs are consumable items that can be bought repeatedly. Purchased coins are recorded in your save, so they can't be restored with the App Store's "Restore Purchases".

5. External links

The Game contains a link to our Facebook page. An external website opens in your browser only when you tap it, and that site applies its own privacy policy.

6. Children

The Game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. It contains cartoon-style fighting; see the App Store age rating for the suitable age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, email arcconceptgames@gmail.com.
